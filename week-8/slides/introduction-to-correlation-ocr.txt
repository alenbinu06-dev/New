=== p4 (OCR)
Correlation - Properties
· Direction: Correlation can be positive (both variables move in the
same direction) or negative (one variable moves up while the other
moves down).
 Strength: The strength of the relationship is measured on a scale from
-1 to 1. A correlation of 1 indicates a perfect positive relationship, -1
indicates a perfect negative relationship, and 0 indicates no
relationship.
