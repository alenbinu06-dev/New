So what?
Imagine you're creating a new kind of material to use in buildings. You're
checking how strong it is by doing strength tests
E.g. Strength data (in units) collected from 30 tests = 23 + 25 + 26 + 28 + 24
+ 25+26+27+ 29+25+ 26+ 28+24+ 26+25+ 800+ 25+28+ 26+27
+26+25+26+28+27+25+26+24+25+26=1551
Average = the sum (1551) divided by the total count of numbers (30))= 1551
/30~51.17
Since the data set has 30 values, the median is the middle value when the
data is arranged in order. In this case, the median is the 15th value, which is
26.
Skewed results make the material appear stronger
than it truly is. As engineers be careful with
extreme results - as they will directly impact on
the safety & reliability of building design.
