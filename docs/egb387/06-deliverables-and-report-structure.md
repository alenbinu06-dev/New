# 6. Deliverables, report structure and submission mechanics

## 6.1 Assessment particulars

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Weighting | **25%** of the unit |
| Assessment type | Group |
| Split | **Group 15%**, **Individual 10%** — both a group and an individual submission |
| Group size | **4 or 5** members, finalised **by week 2 at the latest** |
| Group formation | Discuss with classmates, agree a group of 4–5, then all members join **the same Canvas self sign-up group**. **Do not join any group randomly without prior agreement from the group members.** |
| Work period | **7 weeks**, producing a **project executive plan** |
| Progress reporting | **Weekly progress reporting is expected as part of the submission**; contribution is assessed via **engagement with the group** |
| Due | **11 September 2026, 23:59** |
| Format | **Word format**, submitted **electronically via Canvas** |

## 6.2 Part 1 — Internal Briefing Document (GROUP submission)

A briefing document **suited for internal company use in preparing the tender**. The briefing
specifies these sections:

1. **The supervision team** proposed for the project, with **the duration of each senior person
   onsite nominated**. **The availability of each is to be addressed and any deficiencies
   resolved.**
2. **A preliminary construction program** indicating the **timing and duration of all significant
   phases** of the work and the **interrelationship of tasks**.
3. **A plan of the temporary roads, work areas and the deployment of site buildings** proposed.
4. **A detailed description of the methods** adopted for the areas of construction:
   - Construction of the **rolled concrete dam including obtaining the materials**
   - Construction of the **Power and Pump Station**
   - Construction of the **concrete access bridge**
   - Construction of the **pipeline**

### Sketch requirement (non-negotiable)

> "The text of your submission is to be supported by sketches. These sketches **may be freehand**
> if desired but **must be clear and orderly**. **Submissions without adequate sketches will be
> deemed incomplete and will be rejected.**"

## 6.3 Group report format guide (from the Guide to Group Component)

The guide is explicit that it is "**only to order your thinking**... **It is not to be slavishly
followed.**"

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum length | **12 pages** |
| Page size | **A4** |
| Style | **Not a formal report with references** — it is a document responding to a request that you decide the construction procedures in advance of a bid being compiled by your company's estimators |
| Sketches | **Either hand sketches or electronic means** where appropriate are acceptable |

### Prescribed chronological order

- **Preamble** — a short description of the Project and the construction issues you have
  identified. **About 300 words.**
- **1.** A clear **scope statement**, **value proposition (client's and contractor's)**, and
  **proposed construction methodology adopted**.
- **2. Preliminary Construction Program.**
- **3. Construction methods (select ONE to present), including formwork as required**, from:
  - Construction of the rolled concrete dam including obtaining the materials
  - Construction of the power and pump station
  - Design and construction of the concrete access bridge
  - Construction of the pipelines
- **4. Stakeholders, including Aboriginal group consideration.**
- **Appendix** — sub-programs (support for the consolidated submission above).
- **Sign off and date — all students in the group.**
- **Informal appendix** (not part of the report and **not formally marked**) — any rough documents
  used in compiling the report that you believe may offer further explanation.

## 6.4 Part 2 — Reflective Report (INDIVIDUAL submission)

Prepared **on completion and submission of the group document**. Each member reflects **generally
on the processes adopted by you and your group in decision making**, and **in addition to the
generic approach** must specifically address the way decisions were made in **one** of these areas:

1. The **composition of the supervisory team**.
2. The **provision of materials for the dam construction**.
3. The **method recommended for the underwater work** associated with construction of the Power
   and Pump Station.
4. The **reasoning behind your adoption of temporary works**.

Rules:

- **Each member of the group is to select one** of the above topics for an expanded discussion on
  the thought process used in arriving at the decisions for that task.
- **Each report is to be certified as being the individual work of that member by a signed
  statement to that effect.**
- Length: **in the order of 2–3 pages**.

### How it is judged

> "In assessing the quality of this section, **evidence of a clear line of reasoning in arriving at
> your chosen methods will be the principal criteria**. **It will not matter that you did not adopt
> a method that could be expected of a seasoned constructor** — this will come with experience. An
> **honest appraisal** of the process you used will serve to reinforce the lessons you have learned."

And from the individual guide preamble:

> "To be of benefit **honest answers are required — no penalty will result**. **Self serving answers
> become obvious and will act to weaken your position.**"
> "**Do not use the questions as a questionnaire — rather as a framework to order your thoughts.**"

## 6.5 The individual report prompt framework

Four prompt groups are supplied. They map onto the two individual CRA criteria as shown.

### Initial exploration → CRA criterion 1

- Did **everyone have a say**, or was the discussion **dominated by one member** — perhaps one with
  some experience in the area? (This is **not uncommon and may well lead to the best decision**.)
- If this was the case, **did it cause concern in the group**?
- **How much investigation of the supplied documentation** was undertaken **before the first option
  emerged**?
- Did you **seek information outside the lecture and tutorial program**?
- Did you feel **comfortable moving outside the methods discussed** in lectures and tutorials?

### Selection process → CRA criterion 1

- Did you **adopt your first option immediately** as it was clearly the best?
- Did you **explore further options** even though you were certain you would adopt the first option?
- Was the chosen option **critically explored by all members**?
- **At what stage did you consider how allied tasks might impinge** on the method?

### Mechanics of the selection → CRA criterion 2

- How much did **sketching and quantitative analysis** assist you?
- To what extent was the **company's available equipment a determinant** in your choice?

### Review process → CRA criterion 2

- Did you **subsequently change your decision** in light of a more comprehensive understanding of
  the **interrelationship to other major tasks**?
- **Would you change anything substantially** if you were to undertake the exercise again?
