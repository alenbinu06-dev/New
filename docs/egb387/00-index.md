## Headline facts (quick reference)

- Assessment weighting: **25% of the unit** — group component **15%**, individual component **10%**.
- Submission deadline: **11 September 2026, 23:59**, Word format, via Canvas.
- Group size: **4 or 5 members**, finalised by **week 2** at the latest, via Canvas self sign-up.
- Group report length: **maximum 12 pages**, A4.
- Individual report length: **order of 2–3 pages**.
- Project award: **end of March 2027**. Completion required by: **31 May 2029**.
- Working hours: **10 hours/day**, **no noise nuisance after 6:00 pm**, **6-day week** except in emergencies.
- Sketches are **mandatory**: "Submissions without adequate sketches will be deemed incomplete and will be rejected."
- **The use of GenAI tools is prohibited for this assessment.** See `08-conflicts-gaps-and-checklist.md`.

- **ULO 2** — Demonstrate the importance of connectivity of project time, cost, risk and quality.
