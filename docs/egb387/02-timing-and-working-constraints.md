# 2. Timing and working constraints

All items in section 2.1 are stated verbatim in the briefing under "Additional Information —
Timing". Section 2.2 contains arithmetic derived from those statements and is labelled as such.

## 2.1 Stated constraints

| Constraint | Value |
|---|---|
| Project award | **End of March 2027** |
| Required completion | **31 May 2029** |
| Environmental approvals | **All secured** (no approval risk to program) |
| Working hours | **Limited to 10 hours per day** |
| Noise curfew | Hours are to be arranged to ensure **no noise nuisance occurs after 6:00 pm** |
| Working week | **Restricted to six days per week, except in emergency situations** |

## 2.2 Derived figures (calculated, not stated in the briefing)

- Available construction window: **1 April 2027 → 31 May 2029 = 791 calendar days ≈ 113 weeks
  ≈ 26 months**.
- At 6 days/week: **≈ 678 available working days** before deducting public holidays, wet weather
  and shutdowns.
- At 10 h/day × 6 days: **60 working hours per week** maximum.
- The 6:00 pm noise curfew combined with a 10-hour day effectively forces a day shift roughly in
  the band **07:00–18:00** (allowing for breaks), i.e. **no night shift** is available for
  noise-generating work. Double-shifting is therefore not a legitimate schedule-recovery lever for
  noisy activities — a key programming constraint.

## 2.3 Program-relevant interactions to keep in view

- **Plant lead times** (see `04-plant-and-materials-registers.md`) run from 2 days to 40 days for
  inspection/transport/assembly and must be laid over the front end of the program.
- **Key personnel availability dates** (see `03-company-personnel-and-capacity.md`) all fall
  *before* the March 2027 award except where noted, but the **Earthworks Foreman is only available
  end January 2027** and only **one of the two required Project Engineers is available**.
- The **dredging, electrical and mechanical** works are carried out by **Client-nominated
  companies** whose activities you must coordinate — their interfaces are program constraints you
  do not fully control.
- Water level control at the Pump Station (average water level RL 75.0, invert RL 70.0) implies
  in-water/underwater working windows that must be programmed around; see
  `05-technical-data-drawings.md`.
