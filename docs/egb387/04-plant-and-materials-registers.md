Why this register matters for method selection:

- The **1900 m of sheet piling plus 4 sheetpile corners** is the natural material for a
  **cofferdam** enclosing the Pump/Power Station excavation in the water — and the company also
  owns the **sheetpile crib**, **both pile hammers**, and the **pile frame**.
- The **610 × 230 UB, 330 m** and **second hand marine falsework sections** are the natural
  material for **temporary works** — bridge falsework, cofferdam walings/struts, barge bridging,
  trestles.
- The **400 dia × 10 mm tube, 100 m** matches the **steel tube piles** shown on the Pump Station
  drawing and matches the **pile helmet** in the owned plant register.
- Sheet piling and corners are at **Emerald**, and the salvaged UB at **Gladstone** — long road
  haulage from Central Queensland to Brisbane, so transport duration and permits are a real
  program input, and 145 t of sheet piling is a substantial number of loads.
- The steel beams are noted as "Available **July**" with no year stated in the source document.

## 4.4 Capacity observations relevant to method choice

- Heaviest owned lift capability is **16 t @ 20 m** (two 30 m lattice-jib mobile cranes). Anything
  heavier or at greater radius requires the hired **150 t tracked crane** (30 days lead) or the
  hired **25 t telescopic mobile crane**.
- There is **no owned concrete batch plant** — concrete supply must be either the **hired 5 m³
  batch plant (30 days lead, ex Gladstone)** with the **two hired 5 m³ agitator trucks (20 days)**,
  or externally supplied. For a **rolled compacted concrete dam** the production rate of a single
  5 m³ plant is a central feasibility question for the program.
- Marine/in-water capability is entirely owned: **2 dumb barges (13 × 6 × 1.5 m) with bridging
  beams**, **3 workboats**, **2 dewatering pumps (200 dia)**, **sheetpile crib**, **pile frame**,
  and **both hammers**. This strongly signals the intended approach to the underwater work at the
  Pump/Power Station.
- Earthmoving fleet is deep and owned (2 × D8 with rippers, CAT 613 and 621 scrapers, CAT 320,
  120G grader, 930 loader, 426 backhoe, CAT 815 compactor, Dynapac CA 25 roller, 2 tip trucks),
  matching the company's stated earthworks experience.
