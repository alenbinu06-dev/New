# 7. Marking criteria (both CRAs)

## 7.0 Rule applying to both rubrics

> **"If no information is provided in answer to a question the Mark will be ZERO."**

This makes **coverage the first priority**: every criterion must have a substantive response, and
every sub-element named in a criterion ("all the areas", "all the stakeholders and aboriginal
groups") must be visibly addressed. Silence on any one of them is a zero on that criterion, not a
partial mark.

## 7.4 What the rubrics reward, in plain terms

Reading the band ladders, marks are gained in this order:

1. **Say something on every criterion** (moves 0 → grade 1).
2. **Cover every named area / every stakeholder group** (moves grade 2 → grade 3).
3. **Be logically consistent and factually accurate** (moves grade 3/4 → grade 5).
4. **Justify — give well argued reasons** (moves grade 5 → grade 6).
5. **Cross-link the criteria to each other** (moves grade 6 → grade 7).

Step 5 is the only thing separating a 6 from a 7 and it appears in **every single criterion of both
rubrics**. Concretely, that means the construction method section should reference the program, the
program should reference the supervision team and plant lead times, the stakeholder section should
reference the methods and the program, and the individual reflection should tie the mechanics of
selection back to the exploration and review narrative.

Note also the weighting emphasis in the group rubric: **stakeholders including Aboriginal groups
carries 30 marks — equal to the construction methods criterion and double the construction
program**. It is the single most under-weighted-by-intuition part of this assignment.
