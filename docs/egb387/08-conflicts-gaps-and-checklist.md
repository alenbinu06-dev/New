# 8. Conflicts between documents, open gaps, and a compliance checklist

## 8.1 Academic integrity — read this first

The briefing states, verbatim:

> "You are reminded to maintain academic integrity and professional standards in your submission
> in a manner which is fair, honest, and accountable.
> **The use of artificial intelligence (GenAI) tools are prohibited for the assessment.**"

Consequences for how this knowledge base may be used:

- These notes are a **faithful, reorganised transcription of the five supplied source documents**.
  They contain **no drafted assessment content** — no scope statement, no construction methodology,
  no program, no stakeholder analysis, no reflection text — and none should be added here.
- The report itself, both Part 1 and Part 2, must be **written by the group members**. Part 2
  additionally requires a **signed statement certifying it is the individual work of that member**,
  and Part 1 requires **sign off and date by all students in the group**.
- The individual report is a **reflection on the group's own decision-making process**. Its content
  can only come from what the group actually did; it cannot be sourced from anywhere else and still
  be truthful, which is precisely what the guide asks for ("honest answers are required — no penalty
  will result").

## 8.2 Direct conflicts between the documents

### Conflict 1 — how many construction methods must be presented? (most important)

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| Briefing, Part 1 item 4 | "A detailed description of the methods you would adopt for **the four areas of construction listed below**" — then lists dam, Power and Pump Station, concrete access bridge, pipeline |
| Guide to group component, item 3 | "Construction methods (**select one to present**) including formwork as required" — then lists the same four |
| Group CRA, criterion 3 (30 marks) | "Construction methods including formwork **in one of the area selected**" |

**Two of the three documents, including the rubric that actually assigns the marks, say ONE.** The
briefing says four. The lowest-risk reading is to present **one area in full depth with formwork**
(the marked item), while giving the other three brief method statements so the briefing's
requirement is not left unanswered — noting that the CRA's zero rule bites on *missing* answers,
not on brief ones. **Confirm with the unit coordinator.**

### Conflict 2 — which sections belong in the group report?

| Section | Required by briefing Part 1 | In group guide's chronological order | Marked in group CRA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preamble (~300 words) | — | Yes | (not a separate criterion) |
| Scope statement, value proposition, methodology | — | Yes (item 1) | Yes — 25 marks |
| **Supervision team + duration onsite + availability deficiencies resolved** | **Yes (item 1)** | **No** | **No separate criterion** |
| Preliminary construction program | Yes (item 2) | Yes (item 2) | Yes — 15 marks |
| **Plan of temporary roads, work areas, site buildings** | **Yes (item 3)** | **No** | **No separate criterion** |
| Construction methods | Yes (item 4) | Yes (item 3) | Yes — 30 marks |
| **Stakeholders incl. Aboriginal groups** | Mentioned in "What you need to do", not in the Part 1 section list | **Yes (item 4)** | **Yes — 30 marks** |
| Appendix: sub-programs | — | Yes | — |
| Sign off and date, all students | — | Yes | — |
| Informal appendix | — | Yes (explicitly **not** marked) | — |

**Resolution that satisfies all three documents:** follow the **group guide's chronological order**
(it aligns with the CRA), and fold the two briefing-only items — the **supervision team** and the
**temporary roads / work areas / site buildings plan** — into the methodology and program sections.
This is doubly useful because every CRA criterion awards the top band only for work "**linked to
topics of the other criteria**", and supervision and site layout are natural cross-links.

### Conflict 3 — terminology for the dam

The briefing text calls it a "**rolled concrete dam**"; the drawings label it "**ROLLED COMPACTED
DAM**". Both refer to **roller compacted concrete (RCC)** construction. Use one term consistently.

### Conflict 4 — number of individual topics vs. group size

There are **four** individual reflective report topics but groups may have **five** members. The
briefing says "each member of the group is to select one of the above topics" without stating
whether topics must be unique across the group. In a group of five, at least one topic must be
shared unless the coordinator directs otherwise. **Confirm.**

## 8.3 Information gaps in the supplied documents

- **"Steel beams (salvaged) ... Available July"** — no year is stated. Whether this is July 2026
  (pre-award, i.e. no constraint) or July 2027/2028 (mid-construction, i.e. a real constraint on
  temporary works using the 610 × 230 UB) is undetermined.
- **No quantities** are given for excavation, concrete volumes, pipeline length, or dam volume; all
  must be estimated from the conceptual drawings, which are explicitly **not to scale**.
- **No geotechnical depths** are given for the four strata — only their sequence.
- **No pipeline route length** for the penstock and refilling pipeline; only diameters
  (1.5 m penstock, 2.0 m emergency discharge) and the inlet/outlet inverts.
- **No dam foundation profile** along the 250 m crest — only "RL 86 (follows contours)".
- **Weekly progress reporting** is required "as part of the submission" but neither guide provides a
  section for it. The **appendix / informal appendix** is the obvious home. **Confirm.**
- The briefing lists **three Client-nominated companies** (electrical, mechanical, dredging) but
  gives **no names, scopes, durations or interface dates** for them.
- **No page limit** is stated in the briefing for either part; the limits come from the guides
  (group **12 pages**, individual **2–3 pages**).

## 8.4 Compliance checklist

### Part 1 — group submission

- [ ] Group of **4–5**, all in the **same Canvas group**, finalised by **week 2**
- [ ] **Maximum 12 pages**, **A4**, **Word format**
- [ ] **Preamble ~300 words** — project description + construction issues identified
- [ ] **Scope statement**
- [ ] **Value proposition — client's AND contractor's** (both, explicitly)
- [ ] **Proposed construction methodology adopted**
- [ ] **Preliminary construction program** — timing, duration, **and interrelationship of tasks**
- [ ] **Construction method for the selected area, including formwork**
- [ ] **Supervision team**, with **duration of each senior person onsite** and **availability
      deficiencies resolved** (the missing second Project Engineer)
- [ ] **Plan of temporary roads, work areas and site buildings**
- [ ] **Stakeholders including Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander groups**, with **reasons AND
      actions** (30 marks — the top band needs both)
- [ ] **Sketches throughout** — freehand acceptable, must be clear and orderly. **A submission
      without adequate sketches is rejected.**
- [ ] **Appendix: sub-programs**
- [ ] **Sign off and date by every group member**
- [ ] Optional **informal appendix** (not marked)
- [ ] **Every criterion has a substantive answer** — no blanks (zero rule)
- [ ] Sections **cross-reference each other** (the only route to the grade 7 band)

### Part 2 — individual submission

- [ ] **2–3 pages**
- [ ] Generic reflection on the group's decision-making processes
- [ ] **One selected topic** from the four, discussed in expanded depth
- [ ] Covers **initial exploration** and **selection process** (CRA criterion 1, 50 marks)
- [ ] Covers **mechanics of selection** and **review process** (CRA criterion 2, 50 marks)
- [ ] **Signed statement certifying it is the individual work of that member**
- [ ] Honest rather than self-serving — explicitly rewarded, and "self serving answers become
      obvious"

### Both

- [ ] Submitted **electronically via Canvas** in **Word format** by **11 September 2026, 23:59**
- [ ] **No GenAI used** in producing the assessment
