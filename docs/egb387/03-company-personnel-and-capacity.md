# 3. Your company, capacity and personnel

## 3.1 Your company

- A **medium sized civil construction company** located at **Sumner Park, Brisbane**.
- Experience: **earthworks** and **complex structures, particularly concrete structures**.
- Delivery model: the company undertakes **as much work as practicable by directly employing
  labour**, using **sub-contractors only for**:
  - **building trade functions associated with the Pump and Power Station**, and
  - **curtain grouting to the dam cut off**.

This is a hard constraint on method selection: any proposal that leans on subcontracting outside
those two named packages runs against the company's stated delivery model.

## 3.2 Assessment of the company's capacity to undertake the work

The briefing lists four capacity headings:

1. The outlay on **capital expenditure for equipment is small**.
2. **Other company commitments are not excessive** through the period.
3. **Availability of suitable company personnel** (see 3.3).
4. **Availability of company equipment**, **company owned salvaged materials**, and
   **externally hired plant** (see `04-plant-and-materials-registers.md`).

Point 1 reinforces that the solution should lean on **owned plant and salvaged materials** first,
and on **hire** rather than purchase where owned plant is insufficient.

## 3.3 Availability of suitable company personnel

| Role | Availability |
|---|---|
| **Project Manager** | Completes current project **November 2026** |
| **Project Engineers (2 required)** | **Only 1 available October 2026** |
| **Earthworks Foreman** | Available **end January 2027** |
| **Concreting Foreman** | Available **end October 2026** |
| **General Foreman** | Can be made available **October 2026** |
| **Design Office capacity** | **Adequate after October 2026** |

### The one explicit personnel deficiency

- **Two Project Engineers are required but only one is available.** The briefing requires that
  "the availability of each is to be addressed and **any deficiencies resolved**" — so the second
  Project Engineer is a gap the group must explicitly close (e.g. recruit, second from another
  project, promote, or contract) and justify.

### Notes on the dates

- All listed availabilities precede the **end of March 2027 award**, so no listed person is a
  start-up blocker on the award date — with the exception that resourcing decisions for the
  **missing second Project Engineer** need lead time.
- The **Design Office is adequate only after October 2026**, which matters because the **access
  bridge is a Design and Construct component** requiring in-house design effort.
- Tender/pre-award work occurs before the award, i.e. in the **late-2026 window**, when the PM
  (Nov 2026) and Design Office (after Oct 2026) become free.

## 3.4 Supervision deliverable

The briefing requires the group report to nominate **the supervision team proposed for the
project with the duration of each senior person onsite nominated**, and to address the
availability of each and resolve any deficiencies. This links directly to:

- the **preliminary construction program** (who is needed when), and
- **individual reflective report topic 1** — "The composition of the supervisory team".
