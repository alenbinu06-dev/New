## Topic and research question

- **Problem:**
  - Recycled concrete aggregate (RCA) carries a porous layer of old mortar. That raises water absorption and weakens recycled aggregate concrete (RAC), which limits RAC to non-structural uses.
  - CO2 curing (accelerated carbonation) precipitates calcium carbonate in that mortar layer, densifying the interfacial transition zone (ITZ) and storing CO2.
- **Research question:** once the CO2 concentration during curing is properly controlled, does adding a co-treatment agent (alkaline slurry or biochar) still meaningfully improve RAC? Or does controlled CO2 curing alone achieve comparable results?
- **Gap identified:** no study varies carbonation intensity and agent presence together on the same aggregate and mix design. It is therefore unclear whether the agent adds a mechanism of its own or only makes up for weak carbonation.
